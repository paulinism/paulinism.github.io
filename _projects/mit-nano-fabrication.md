One-week visiting-student intensive in semiconductor device fabrication at **MIT.nano** (MIT, Cambridge, USA), October 2025, covering three full fabrication modules in Class 100/1K/10K cleanrooms under strict PPE/safety protocols.

**Modules**

- **Solar cell:** wafer preparation and LPCVD hydrogenated silicon nitride (HFSIN) anti-reflective coating deposition (~32 nm/min)
- **MEMS cantilevers & bridges:** LPCVD low-stress silicon nitride (MFSIN), RCA clean, photolithography (130 mJ/cm² exposure, 15 µm beam width), SF₆ reactive-ion etching, and KOH beam release — both cantilevers and bridges passed mechanical force-displacement testing
- **Microfluidic diffusion mixer:** PDMS soft lithography and plasma bonding; ran tracer-particle flow tests (an injection-pump clog eventually blocked the channel — a real, documented failure mode)

**My contribution**

- Executed thin-film deposition (PECVD/PVD), photolithography, and wet/dry etching steps across all three modules
- Calculated refractive index (n ≈ 2.02) from etch/interference data
- Wore full cleanroom bunny-suit PPE for KOH and other hazardous chemical steps
- Co-authored a technical report on the three modules' fabrication processes with a teammate, under Dr. Javier Izquierdo Reyes and Dr. Arnoldo Salazar Soto (Tecnológico de Monterrey / MIT)
