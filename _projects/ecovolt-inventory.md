Automated inventory and PPE (personal protective equipment) tracking system built for EcoVolt CCM's paddock operations at Shell Eco-marathon competitions, where tools and safety equipment are shared across a 30+ person team under time pressure.

**My contribution**

- Designed the QR-code-based item identification and loan workflow (request → approval → tracking → return)
- Built the web platform for scanning items, registering loans, and tracking item/loan status in real time
- Implemented automated overdue-loan alerts to admins and borrowers
- Digitized the equipment inventory (CSV import with ID, category, and location per item)

**Context:** Escudería EcoVolt CCM, Shell Eco-marathon (paddock operations), 2025–2026.
**Result:** Faster equipment handoffs and complete return tracking during international competitions, supporting EcoVolt's HSSE (Health, Safety, Security, Environment) compliance.
**Stack:** QR codes, computer vision, web app (loan/approval workflow), CSV-based inventory data
