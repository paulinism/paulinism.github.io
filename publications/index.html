Conference paper

IEEE SAIBOT 2026 · Submitted

Real-Time Telemetry and Computational Intelligence Pipeline for Energy-Efficient Driving of an Electric Eco-Marathon Vehicle

P. L. Ruíz-Servín, J. D. González-Fernández, A. Flores-Mendoza, J. Izquierdo-Reyes

IEEE Latin American School on Computational Intelligence and Robotics (SAIBOT), 2026 — first author

Three generations of telemetry hardware for an electric Shell Eco-marathon prototype — current, voltage, GPS and motor data over CAN, logged to SD and streamed live — and a data pipeline that compares driving strategies, including a simulator bench test where constant throttle beat pulse-and-glide by 14 %.
